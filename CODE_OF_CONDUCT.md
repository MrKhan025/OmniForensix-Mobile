# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the `OmniForensix-Mobile` community a harassment-free, constructive, and ethical digital forensics engineering experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

* Focusing on forensically sound, defensive, post-acquisition analysis techniques.
* Respecting privacy, evidentiary integrity, and applicable laws across jurisdictions.
* Providing constructive technical feedback on pull requests and artifact parser implementations.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or unethical behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the repository maintainer (`@MrKhan025`).
